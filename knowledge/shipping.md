# NovaShop Shipping Policy

## Standard Shipping

- Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days from the date of order confirmation.
- Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.
- Standard shipping is free for all orders over $50.
- For orders under $50, standard shipping costs $4.99.

## Express Shipping

- Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days from the date of order confirmation.
- Express shipping is available for an additional fee of $12.99.
- Express shipping is not available for PO Box or APO/FPO addresses.

## Order Cancellation After Shipment

- Once an order has shipped, it cannot normally be cancelled.
- Customers who need to cancel a shipped order should contact NovaShop support for exceptional cases.
- In exceptional cases, the customer may be asked to refuse delivery and return the package unopened.

## International Shipping

- NovaShop currently ships to the United States and Canada only.
- International shipping outside the US and Canada is not available at this time.
- Shipping times to Canada are 5 to 7 business days (standard) or 2 to 3 business days (express).

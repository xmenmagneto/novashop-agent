# NovaShop Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return Window

- Most items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.
- Items purchased during a sale or promotion have the same 30-day return window.

## Return Conditions

- Items must be in their original condition, unused, and with all tags and packaging intact.
- Personal care items, final-sale items, and gift cards cannot be returned.

## Refunds

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.
- Original shipping charges are non-refundable, unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

## Return Shipping

- Customers are responsible for return shipping costs.
- NovaShop provides a prepaid return label for defective or incorrect items at no charge to the customer.

## Exchanges

- Exchanges are available for the same item in a different size or color, subject to stock availability.
- Exchange requests must be made within 30 days of delivery.

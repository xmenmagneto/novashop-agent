# NovaShop Frequently Asked Questions

## How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number. You can use this number on the carrier's website to track your package.

## How long does standard shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

## What is NovaShop's return policy?

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery, provided they are in original condition. See the Returns policy for full details.

## Do you offer free shipping?

Standard shipping is free for orders over $50. Orders under $50 have a $4.99 standard shipping fee.

## How do I contact customer support?

You can contact NovaShop customer support through the chat widget on the website or by emailing support@novashop.example.

## What payment methods do you accept?

NovaShop accepts major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay.

## How long does a refund take?

Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

## Do you ship internationally?

NovaShop ships to the United States and Canada. International shipping to other countries is not available at this time.

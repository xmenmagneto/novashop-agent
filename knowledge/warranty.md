# NovaShop Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty

- All NovaShop products come with a 1-year limited warranty from the date of purchase.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.

## What the Warranty Covers

- Defects in materials or workmanship under normal use.
- Replacement or repair of defective parts at NovaShop's discretion.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover

- Damage from misuse, accidental damage, or unauthorized repairs.
- Normal wear and tear.
- Damage caused by water, fire, or other environmental factors.
- Consumable parts such as batteries, filters, or light bulbs.

## Making a Warranty Claim

- To make a warranty claim, contact NovaShop support with your order number and a description of the defect.
- Proof of purchase is required for all warranty claims.
- Warranty claims must be made within the warranty period.

## Extended Warranty

- An optional 2-year extended warranty is available for purchase at checkout for eligible products.
